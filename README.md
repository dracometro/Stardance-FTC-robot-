Context 
FTC or First Tech Challenge is a robotics challenge where highschoolers work in teams to play a game against other teams. The game they play is a rotating game which changes every year and is announced slightly after the start of school and ends in March. This robot was made in 9 days and is meant to be a test for what we can do. The game this year is to shoot 2.8 and 3.6 in balls 5 ft(eww imperial) in the air. 
<br>
<br>
Design Notes 
This robot took roughly 10 hours to design from beginning to end. I will for the sake of documenting split it into 3 parts:  drivetrain, intake, and shooter. I will also give build instructions while explaining each part and if you wish to also build this you can do it in the order I introduce parts

On the inside the drivetrain is not complicated 
The mecanum wheels (the wheels with weird diagonal rollers) let the robot move forward and backward as well as side to side letting it have something called holonomic movement which means the robot can move more efficiently and move around defence.  The drive train is split into two of these panels as shown by image. Then the panels are connected by a back plate 
<br>

*builders note if you want to make this start with the motors and motor plate because it will be more convenient to add while the plates are not together. Also assemble both the left and right sides separately then combine with back plate *

Shooter 
<br><br>
The shooter is arguably the most important part of the robot (it actually scores points.) The shooter is the simplest part of the robot, It is a simple hooded shooter from previous FTC games which lets the ball come out at a variable angle because of the “hood” of the shooter rotating meaning it can shoot accurately from anywhere.
